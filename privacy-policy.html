DunePulse: Orb Runner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

App: DunePulse: Orb Runner (package com.ironcipherpoint.dunepulseorbrunner)
Developer: Quartz Nerve Center

DunePulse: Orb Runner is a free, offline arcade game. We respect your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app does and does not handle. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

1. Information We Collect

DunePulse: Orb Runner does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. Specifically, the app does not collect or process:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any account information;
  • Location data;
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data;
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers (no Advertising ID is used);
  • Usage analytics, crash analytics, or behavioral tracking;
  • Payment or financial information.

2. Local Gameplay Data

The app stores a small amount of gameplay data — such as your best scores — locally on your device, using the device's private app storage. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and is not accessible to other apps. You can permanently delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Internet Access and Third Parties

DunePulse: Orb Runner works fully offline. It does not connect to the internet, does not contain advertising, and does not include third-party analytics, social, payment, or login SDKs. Because the app does not send data anywhere, no data is shared with third parties.

4. Permissions

The app does not request any Android permissions. It does not access the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, notifications, or any account information.

5. Children's Privacy

DunePulse: Orb Runner is intended for teenage and adult audiences (ages 16 and older) and is not directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including minors. Because no data is collected or transmitted, no personal information can be gathered through the app.

6. Data Security

Since DunePulse: Orb Runner does not transmit any user data, your gameplay records stay on your own device under the protection of your device's operating system. There is no remote server, database, or cloud component associated with the app.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay records remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. There is no online copy of this data for us to retain or delete.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
